## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. 💳 Credit Card Payment Defaulters

Power BI dashboard made to find credit card payment defaulters by **age and customer ID**. SQL was used to clean the data and upload it into Power BI, and Python with the Matplotlib library was used for visualization. It tracks late payments and the debt to income ratio, with a city wise split and filters for customer ID, age and credit history years.

**Tools:** `Power BI` `SQL` `Python` `Matplotlib`

### 2. 🛒 Amazon Sales Dashboard

Looker Studio dashboard connected to a **Google Sheet**. It tracks actual revenue, profit, actual cost and expenditure by product and by country, compares payment gateways to see which one suits customers best, and uses a world map to show sales revenue by country.

**Key insights:**
- Reduce shipping cost to protect profit.
- Focus on the fastest payment gateways, since customers prefer them for payment.
- Track average monthly sales by product first, and keep stock available.
- Offer at least a 10% discount on health products to increase their sales.

**Tools:** `Looker Studio` `Google Sheets`

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20on-GitHub-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/amu5236/Amazon-Sales-Dashboard)
